## Game description — ready to paste

**The walls have eyes. Close them, and a passage appears. But another eye wakes somewhere else.**

BLINK MAZE is a quiet puzzle game about seeing a different way forward. A single blink swaps every blue and amber wall in the maze. Moves are unlimited, but your blinks are precious.

Play11 small, handcrafted mazes: five first steps and six discoveries. Sometimes a longer walk is the cleverer route. Sometimes the best move is to leave your eyes alone.

- Arrows / WASD: move one tile
- Space: BLINK
- Z: undo a move or blink, restoring its budget
- R: restart the current maze
- On-screen buttons also work on mobile
- EN / JA: change language
- Sound button: turn music and effects on or off (starts muted)

No timer, enemies or game over. Try a different route whenever you like. Three optional hint steps support the discovery mazes; each needs an explicit reveal. Any maze can be selected freely.

### Trijam #393
